# Review round 1: findings and resolutions

Three review agents were run on v2 of this change (commit 9979768) on 2026-11-05, at the user's request:

> "spin ana agent to falsify the whole specification and another to grind over the specification gaps and third to grind over the overall architecture - all of them must report problems and better sollutions that must be included here in the opens spec takss and planning and tasks"

- Falsifier: F-01–F-25.
- Gap review: G-01–G-20.
- Architecture review: A-01–A-16.

## How to read the Verified column

- **Re-verified.** The main session re-ran the probe or re-fetched the source and saw the output. The evidence is under `research/probes/review/`.
- **Agent evidence.** Accepted on the reviewing agent's own document reading or probe, which the main session did not re-run. These are gaps until re-run; each has a task that will exercise it.
- **Rejected.** Not applied, with the reason.
